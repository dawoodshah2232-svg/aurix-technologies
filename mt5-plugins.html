Deep dives

How the key modules work

Set exposure limits by symbol, group or whole book. When a limit is hit, the module blocks new exposure and alerts your dealing desk — with every action logged.

Credit is issued against clear rules: verified account, first deposit, trading volume. Abuse patterns are flagged before credit is released.

Daily and weekly book summaries: exposure by symbol, client concentration and P&L attribution — exportable for finance and compliance.

Multi-level commissions calculated from real trading activity, approved in the portal and paid on your schedule — reconciled automatically.
